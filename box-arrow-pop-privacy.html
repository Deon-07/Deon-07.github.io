Privacy Policy

Box Arrow POP!

Package Name: com.boxarrowpop.game • Effective Date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Box Arrow POP! (also referred to as "Arrow Pop!", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), available on the Google Play Store under package name com.boxarrowpop.game.

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our players. This policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Box Arrow POP!.

1. Information We Collect

Summary: We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) directly on our own servers.

A. Local Game Data:

Your game progress (levels unlocked, stars earned, high scores, audio settings, booster inventory) is stored locally on your device using Android's private internal storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

B. Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party Services:

To support the free availability of the game, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob). These third-party services may automatically collect certain device and network information, including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address and general geographic location (country/city level)
  • In-game ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The game uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or personalize ads. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used in the app:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads Privacy & Terms

3. Device Permissions Used

Box Arrow POP! requests the following permissions solely to enable game features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load advertisements and fetch ad inventory from Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify internet connectivity before attempting to load ad content.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., button taps, combo pops, level completion).

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Box Arrow POP! does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the EEA/UK). We configure third-party advertising partners to comply with child-directed treatment standards where applicable.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party services through our App without consent, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You can control or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of interest-based ads directly from your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID

6. Security

We value your trust. Because all your game progression is kept locally on your device and no personal accounts are maintained, your personal data is protected by your device's built-in Android sandboxing and security features.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage players to review this page periodically for any updates.
