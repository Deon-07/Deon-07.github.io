Privacy Policy for Daily Tracker

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Daily Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

  • Account Data: If you sign in via Google Sign-In or Supabase authentication, we store your email address and profile name securely to sync your goals, streaks, and focus records across devices.
  • Local Data: Your daily habits, expenses, focus goals, and tracker entries are saved locally on your device and synced with your account database securely.

2. Device Permissions & Usage Access

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Our app requests Usage Access permission strictly to calculate daily screen time and track your focus hours. This data remains on your device and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver daily reminder alerts and streak notifications on your device.
  • Calendar Access: Optional access to view or add calendar events related to your schedules.

3. Advertising & Analytics

We display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ads and measure ad performance in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more details, visit: Google Privacy & Terms

4. Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data stored locally on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing application data or uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Daily Tracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@dailytracker.app
