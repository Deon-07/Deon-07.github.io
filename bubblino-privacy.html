Privacy Policy

Bubblino: Bubble Pop Shooter

Package: com.bubblino.game • Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Summary: Bubblino is a free casual bubble puzzle game. We do not collect or store your personal identity information (such as real name, email, or contacts). Game save progress is stored locally on your device. Non-personal advertising identifiers are handled by trusted ad networks (ironSource / Unity Ads) to deliver video ads and grant in-game rewards.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Bubblino: Bubble Pop Shooter developed by Deon Apps & Games. We are committed to transparency and respect your privacy while playing our game.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Game Storage (On-Device)

All your puzzle progress—including level completion, high scores, collected gems, coins, boosters, and unlocked worlds—is stored locally on your device storage (user://game_save.cfg). We do not transmit your game state to external database servers.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

When you play Bubblino with an active internet connection, third-party advertising and platform SDKs may collect certain non-personal device information:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Network Data: IP address, network carrier, and connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile).
  • Ad Performance: Ad views, clicks, and rewarded video completion callbacks to unlock in-game lives and gems.

3. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

Bubblino integrates industry-standard monetization and platform SDKs:

  • ironSource (Unity LevelPlay Mediation): Used for ad mediation and serving rewarded video / interstitial ads.
    Privacy Policy: https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/
  • Unity Ads: Used for commercial video ad delivery.
    Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for Android platform distribution and game runtime.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Permissions Used

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to connect to ad networks to load video commercials and rewards.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the game to check whether Wi-Fi or cellular network is available before loading ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play permission allowing ad networks to use the advertising identifier for fraud prevention and frequency capping.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Bubblino is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe personal information of a minor has been collected, please contact us for prompt deletion.

6. Managing Ad Preferences

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertisements anytime in your Android settings:

Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
